## 2. Description of Data Processing

We attach great importance to the protection of user privacy and consistently adhere to the principle of “minimum necessity” in all data processing activities.

The above information is used solely to maintain application functionality and does not involve your personal sensitive information.

Except for the explicitly stated scenarios above, the Application will not collect, upload, or synchronize any of your data over the network. All your files and information are stored locally on your device.
