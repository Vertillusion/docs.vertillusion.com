## 2. Definitions and Interpretation

### 2.1 Definition of Terms
1. **"Pogget Inspiration Hub"**: Refers to the digital content sharing platform built into the Pogget computer execution program, providing users with comprehensive online services including uploading, displaying, disseminating, and downloading custom components.
2. **"Custom Component"**: Refers to a digital work developed by the user through technical specifications recognized by the Platform, capable of realizing specific functions within the Pogget program runtime environment, including but not limited to functional modules, interface themes, special effects materials, etc.
3. **"Feishu Document System"**: Refers to the user information collection and processing platform adopted by the Company, used for the standardized collection, desensitization processing, and categorized storage of user-submitted content.
4. **"User Identity Identifier"**: Refers to a digitally encrypted and obfuscated identity recognition code automatically embedded by the Platform's technical system after a component passes review, used for component traceability and copyright protection.
